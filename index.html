        QR maker is my QR code generator, it runs entirely in the browser. It makes minimal codes, codes with a logo in the middle, pixel art codes that spell a word, and dotted codes drawn over a picture. Advanced settings let you pick the error correction level, version and mask. The learn view takes any code apart step by step: bits, error correction blocks, placement, masks and format information, and lets you break it. It has presets for URLs, Wi-Fi, email, SMS and vCards. Download as SVG, PNG or WebP.
        
      
        Every code is decoded again in your browser before it is shown. Nothing leaves your device.
        
      
        Provided free of charge, without tracking. For the love of the game.
